These terms cover Drawn to Scale, a self-hosted tool that generates short explanatory videos from public data and uploads them to a single TikTok account operated by its developer.

Who may use it

The software is operated solely by its developer for a single TikTok account. It is not offered as a service to the public, has no user accounts, and accepts no registrations. Nothing here grants a right to use the software or the name.

What it does

The app reads publicly available news and data sources, composes an animated video and a written caption describing what it shows, and uploads the result to the operator's own TikTok account as a draft. A person reviews each draft before it is published.

Accuracy of content

Videos are generated automatically from public sources, and every figure shown is checked against the source text that produced it. Even so, the content is provided without warranty of accuracy or completeness. Each video's caption names its source so that any claim can be checked directly.

The app uses TikTok's official APIs and is bound by TikTok's own Terms of Service and Community Guidelines. It uploads only content it has generated itself. It does not scrape TikTok, automate engagement, operate multiple accounts, or attempt to influence distribution by artificial means.
