Drawn to Scale

Short explanatory videos built from public data. Every frame is drawn by code from a named source, with no stock footage and no generated imagery.

What it is

Drawn to Scale is a small self-hosted program. It reads the day's public news and data feeds, picks one story with a shape worth showing, and builds an animated scene that explains it: a world the camera moves through, narrated, with every figure on screen traceable to the article it came from.

The video is a drawing, so the caption is the receipt. Every post names its source and links to it, and any number spoken or shown is checked against that source before the video is rendered.

How it works

  1. Public feeds are scored for stories that can actually be shown rather than read out.
  2. A scene is written for the chosen story: the objects, the quantities, the narration.
  3. The narration is synthesised and measured, and the scene is timed to the real audio.
  4. The finished video is uploaded to the project's own TikTok account for review.

The TikTok integration

The app connects to one TikTok account, @drawntoscaleus, using TikTok's official Login Kit and Content Posting API. It requests only the ability to confirm which account is connected and to place a finished video into that account's drafts. It reads no audience data and uploads nothing it did not generate itself.
